# Storyboard — "Total Relief" nano-magnesio crampi (IT)

3 interchangeable hooks + 16 body clips. Reference ad analyzed: `mamabear_killer.mp4` (59.5s, magnesium body-butter b-roll ad).

**Format rules from the brief:** b-roll + your finished voiceover. No audio, dialogue or lip-sync instructions appear in any prompt, and nobody on screen talks. Product shots say to attach the product photo (`CREMA_TOTALRELIEF.png`).

## 1. Reference-ad blueprint

- **Format.** Vertical 9:16 (360×640). Mixes photoreal handheld smartphone UGC with glossy, hyper-saturated AI 3D "inside the body" illustrations (glowing nerves, capsules, a foot skeleton with neon nerve lines and floating white spheres).
- **Pace.** 28 shots in 59.5s, about 28 cuts/min. Mean shot is 2.1s and median 2.0s. One new visual per caption phrase, no shot held past about 5s.
- **Hook.** In-medias-res product macro: a finger dips into an open jar of white cream, with a bold claim in the captions ("This mineral is being…"). Then fast whip-blur cuts into the mechanism.
- **Cast.** Many different people, never one presenter: a young woman on a sofa, a man in his 40s–50s in a blue polo, a blonde woman 40+ in a yellow blouse, a woman in purple and more. Each appears 1–3 times in short beats: rubbing hands, applying cream to a foot, holding the jar to the lens, typing on a laptop.
- **Mechanism visuals.** Literal 3D metaphors cut against UGC: capsules over glowing neurons, energy tunnels, particles entering a foot. Each is a single simple event.
- **Product.** Appears often (about 8 of 28 shots): finger-dip macro, jar held to the selfie lens, jar on the floor while cream goes on the foot, jar shown in a kitchen. It always looks like real use, never a studio packshot, except for the CTA web page.
- **Captions and transitions (edit stage).** Burned-in captions of 1–3 words in white uppercase heavy sans (Montserrat-Black style) with a black outline, centred at about 65% of frame height. Transitions are motion-blur whip pans plus 1–2-frame white flash frames between sections.
- **Narrative arc.** Hook claim → why the usual way fails → topical mechanism → product reveal and benefits → social proof ("helped over 10,000…") → scarcity CTA ("unfortunately word spread… so click") over a laptop or phone shot.
