# Storyboard — "Spalla / 5 Anni" VSL (IT) — v2

17 b-roll clips, 95.0 s total. Format reference: `mamabear_5years.mp4` — 58.9 s, 9:16, 29 shots, UGC b-roll + voiceover.

**What changed from v1**

1. **Rotating cast, no single protagonist.** Six different people carry the ad, the way the reference does — it reads as "people like you", not one man's biography. Three of them come back once; the rest appear a single time.
2. **17 clips instead of 30, every clip ≥ 4 s.** The four permitted exceptions are the three ANNO cards and the "La soluzione?" reveal. Sentences are merged into groups that share one continuous visual event.
3. **No audio direction.** The voiceover is finished and supplied by the client, so there are no sound, music, or voice sections anywhere in this document. Captions are kept — they are picture, not sound.

**Constant across every clip:** nobody on screen ever speaks or lip-syncs. Every clip is silent b-roll cut under the existing narrator track.

## 1. Reference-ad blueprint

- **Format** — 9:16 vertical, no presenter, no talking head. Entirely b-roll under a voiceover: sourced-looking UGC live action intercut with 3D medical animation.
- **Hook** — 0:00–3.5 s, one unbroken clinical shot (blue-gloved hands examining a bare diabetic foot, bloodied gauze on the table) carrying **two** caption cards: the condition named first, the 5-year time cost second. One shot, multiple captions — the precedent this version leans on heavily.
- **Shot density** — 29 shots in 58.9 s: mean 2.03 s, ~29.5 cuts/min. Hard cuts only, no transitions of any kind anywhere.
- **Two-act grammar** — live-action UGC for the symptom timeline; a hard cut into **3D medical CGI** the moment the VO turns from symptom to mechanism; back to UGC for product and CTA, with one CGI insert for absorption.
- **The "Year N" card device** — three real photographed white cards hand-lettered in marker (`Year 1` black on wood behind blurred leaves, `Year 3` blue on a pale shelf, `Year 5` black under hard blind shadows), 0.6–1.5 s each. Homemade, never a graphic title.
- **Cast** — no recurring protagonist. A rotating cast of different real-looking older people in real homes, each failing at an ordinary task. A couple of faces return once or twice; most appear a single time.
- **Captions** — burned in, **all lowercase**, bold italic grotesque, white with a hard black outline and soft drop shadow, 1–2 lines, 3–6 words per line, centred at ~74 % frame height. One or two keywords per card colour-filled: **red in the hook only**, **yellow everywhere after**. No terminal periods.
- **Structure** — visceral hook → Year 1 / 3 / 5 decline ladder → tone reset → CGI mechanism → product in hand → application → CGI absorption → "no pills" → urgency → tap-the-link → static designed end card.

### Pacing note

At 17 clips the generated cut rate is ~10.7 cuts/min against the reference's ~29.5. That gap is deliberate — it is the cost of the 4-second floor — and it closes in the edit, not in generation. Every clip from 03 onward holds one continuous action long enough to take an internal punch-in or reframe on each new caption card, which is where the reference gets its rhythm anyway. Cutting on the caption changes in clips 03, 05, 09, 10, 12, 13 and 15 alone yields roughly 26 apparent cuts/min from the same 17 generations. Shoot for one extra framing per long clip and the ad will feel like the reference despite having half as many renders.

- **Generate in two batches** so the look holds: all live action together (01–09, 14, 16), then all CGI together (10–13, 15). Fix the seed within each batch.
